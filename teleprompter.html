How to use this teleprompter
Step 1 — Right-click → Open with Live Server (or double-click to open in browser)
Step 2 — Set up your browser tabs: client's current site, your demo, overlay panel
Step 3 — Click a video card below to load that script
Step 4 — Start recording in Loom, then use this window to advance cards as you talk
SPACE or → — Advance to next card
← — Go back one card
Click the card — Also advances to next card
Dots at top — Click any dot to jump to that card directly
ESC — Return to this video selection screen
Restart button — Appears at end of script, resets to card 1
Gold ▶ text = on-screen action to take — do it, don't read it out loud  ·  Faded italic = private reminder tip, never read aloud  ·  White text in quotes = your actual words to say
